Summary: Flow-chemistry process development for photooxygenation of dihydroartemisinic acid is described in the context of a continuous micro-reactor utilizing physically immobilized methylene blue on polystyrene microparticles. The reaction type involves oxygen insertion into the C9 position of dihydroartemisinic acid, with key reactants including dihydroartemisinic acid and molecular oxygen. Solvent is not mentioned in this context. Methylene blue serves as both catalyst and photosensitizer. The products are artemisinin and its derivatives. The reactor type is a continuous micro-reactor with immobilized microparticles, and the ID is not specified. Critical conditions include flow rates of 10 mL/min for dihydroartemisinic acid solution and 5 mL/min for oxygen, residence time of 6 hours, temperature of 25°C, and atmospheric pressure. The outcomes are high conversion (94%) and yield (87%) of artemisinin derivatives with good selectivity.
