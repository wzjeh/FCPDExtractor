 The paragraph discusses the development of a continuous flow microreactor for α-bromination of acetophenone using a flow-chemistry process. The reaction involves bromine as the reactant and an iron(III) catalyst. Acetonitrile is used as solvent, and the product is 4-bromoacetophenone with high selectivity. A microreactor with a 10 mm ID was utilized, operating at atmospheric pressure and room temperature. The flow rate of bromine was set to 2 mL/min, while that of acetonitrile and iron(III) catalyst were both set to 4 mL/min. A residence time of 10 minutes ensured high conversion (95%) and yield (87%).
