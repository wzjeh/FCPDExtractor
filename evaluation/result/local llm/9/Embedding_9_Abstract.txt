Summary: Flow-chemistry process development for p-DEB production uses a continuous stirred tank reactor (CSTR) with a 10 L volume. The reaction is an esterification between acetic acid and ethanol in the presence of a solid catalyst (e.g., Amberlyst-15). Key reactants include acetic acid, ethanol, and water as solvent. Products are p-DEB and water. Critical conditions: flow rate = 20 mL/min, residence time = 4 hours, temperature = 80°C, pressure = atmospheric pressure. Outcomes: conversion of acetic acid to p-DEB is 95%, yield is 76% (lower than state of the art), and selectivity for p-DEB is high at >99%.
